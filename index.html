EPI-II · Overview of study design and descriptive study · Interactive reading

Take a study apart, then put it back together

A companion to the lecture and workshops on study design and descriptive epidemiology.

This reading mixes invented examples, such as Hogwarts and PSU, with real numbers. Every real number comes with a citation. Most figures are interactive, so play with them. I hope it is fun.

By Songyos Rajborirug, M.D., Ph.D. · Version of 8 November 2026

Before class. If you have an hour, read the introduction, then chapters 1 to 3.

In the workshops. Chapter 4 goes with Workshop 1. Chapter 5 goes with Workshop 2.

After class. Review any chapter. Look things up on the components page and in the glossary.

Each chapter stands on its own. “Check yourself” and “Going further” are optional.
